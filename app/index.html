P25 Phase 1 simplex. One frequency. Listen, or send the 1011 Hz test tone.

The HackRF transmits only while push-to-talk is held, for at most 30 seconds, at RF gain 0. Use a frequency you are allowed to transmit on. Keep the radios close together, or put a dummy load on the HackRF.

Checking the HackRF…
Clear voice only (algorithm 80 hex). Phase 2, trunking, and encryption keys are not in this page. Listen plays the decoded clear voice in this browser. Push-to-talk sends the standard 1011 Hz tone, and repeats it until you release. Hold the button for a few seconds after the status says transmitting. The tone starts when the transmitter finishes starting. The Motorola stays quiet unless that channel is on this frequency and this NAC, or its NAC is F7E, and the talkgroup matches. OP25’s live microphone coder does not take a NAC, so this page does not use the mic.
